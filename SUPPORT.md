# Han Tiles Support

Han Tiles is an iPad app for learning to write Chinese characters with Apple Pencil.

## Contact

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to **balas.andrej@icloud.com**. Every message is read,
and you will usually get a reply within a few days.

To report a wrong meaning, reading or stroke order, you can also
[open an issue](https://github.com/andrejbalas/hantiles/issues) here on GitHub.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an Apple Pencil?**
Han Tiles is designed for Apple Pencil. If you don't have one, you can turn on
*Allow writing without pencil* in Settings to write with your finger.

**How do I find a character?**
Type it on the home screen as Chinese characters, pinyin (with or without tone
numbers) or English. You can also search from a photo with the camera button next to
the search field.

**Can I practise traditional characters?**
Yes. In Settings, set *Characters* to *Traditional*. You can switch at any time.

**How do I print or share an exercise?**
Tap the share button on the practice sheet and choose *Share as PDF*. You can then
print it, save it to Files or send it.

**Where are my previous exercises?**
On the home screen, under your previous exercises. Tap one to continue where you left
off. They are stored only on your iPad.

**Does Han Tiles collect my data?**
No. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
