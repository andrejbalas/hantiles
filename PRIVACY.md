# Han Tiles Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Han Tiles is an iPad app for learning to write Chinese characters, made by Andrej Balas
(Croatia). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## In short

Han Tiles does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything you do in the
app stays on your iPad. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## What stays on your iPad

- **Your settings**, such as language, simplified or traditional characters, and Apple
  Pencil options.
- **Your practice history**: the characters you practised and your handwriting, so you
  can continue an exercise later.

This information is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone
else. If you use iCloud or computer backups, it is included in your iPad's backups like
the data of any other app, under Apple's terms. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Camera and photos

You can search for characters by taking a photo or choosing one from your photo
library. The text in the photo is recognised on your iPad using Apple's built-in text
recognition. The photo is not saved by Han Tiles and is never uploaded anywhere. The app
asks for permission before using the camera, and you can change this at any time in the
iPad Settings app. When you choose a photo from your library, Han Tiles only receives
the photo you pick.

## Exporting PDFs

When you export an exercise as a PDF, the file is created on your iPad and goes only
where you choose to send it in the share sheet, such as Files, Mail or a printer.

## Links

The Acknowledgements screen links to the websites of the open-source projects Han Tiles
uses. These open in Safari, and those websites have their own privacy policies.

## Information Apple provides

Apple may share anonymous, aggregated statistics with developers, such as the number of
downloads and, if you have agreed to share them with developers, crash reports. These
do not identify you. See Apple's privacy policy at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

## Children

Han Tiles can be used by people of all ages. Because the app collects no personal data,
it collects none from children.

## Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have rights over your
personal data. Han Tiles does not hold any personal data about you, but you are welcome
to contact us with any questions.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
